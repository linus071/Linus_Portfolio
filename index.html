A chatbot that lets analysts ask questions about engagement data for 300+ users in plain language, built during a four-month investigation of Azure AI Foundry.

Mr. Kato

A web-based virtual patient that medical students interview by voice or text, then debrief with an AI preceptor. Used by 300+ medical students.

  • Engineered the WebRTC service layer for live voice conversations with the patient.
  • Traced a transcript bug to its root cause, concurrent messages arriving out of order, and fixed it with a pipeline that buffers until the user's turn is final, deduplicates on server response ID, and flushes in arrival order.
  • Migrated the frontend from JavaScript to TypeScript, added Zustand state management and a pub/sub event bus, and modularised components for reuse.
